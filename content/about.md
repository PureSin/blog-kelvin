Hi, I'm Kelvin Ma, a software engineer. This is my personal blog, where I write about what I'm learning and building. I used to blog on [Medium](https://kelvinma.medium.com/), and in 2024 I moved to this self-hosted Hugo site so I could own my content and keep it under version control.

## What I write about

- **AI coding tools:** hands-on experience with tools like Claude Code, including what works, what doesn't, and how they change the way software gets built.
- **Personal software:** small tools I build for myself, especially since taking a self-made sabbatical.
- **Georgia Tech OMSCS:** summaries and reviews of the courses I take in the Online Master of Science in Computer Science program.
- **Events and hackathons:** recaps and takeaways from conferences and hack days.
- **Books, finance, and tech:** notes from books and educated guesses about where AI, finance, and tech are heading.

Everything here is my own opinion and doesn't represent any employer.

## Privacy

This is a static site with no accounts, no comments, and no forms, so I don't collect any personal information from you. The site doesn't set its own cookies or run ad trackers. It's hosted on Cloudflare, which may process standard request data (like IP addresses) to serve and protect the site, as described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## For AI agents

Every page on this site is also available as Markdown. Request any URL with the `Accept: text/markdown` header. There's a summary of the site and a list of posts at [/llms.txt](/llms.txt).
